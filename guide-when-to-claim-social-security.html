When Should You Claim Social Security?

You can start Social Security retirement as early as 62 or as late as 70. The age you pick changes your check for life. Here's how to think about it.

At a glance

70%Of your full check if you claim at 62 (born 1960 or later)
124%Of your full check if you wait until 70
3.6%Cost-of-living raise for 2027
  • Your “full retirement age” is 67 if you were born in 1960 or later.
  • Claiming early means a smaller check for life. Waiting means a bigger one.
  • If you live past about 80, waiting usually pays more in total.

2Which age is best for you?

  • Claiming early can make sense if you need the money now, have health problems, or don't expect to live into your 80s.
  • Waiting can make sense if you're healthy, can live on other money for now, or are the higher earner in a married couple. Your spouse may get your bigger check if you die first.

A rough rule: the total you collect evens out around age 80. Live longer than that, and waiting wins.

3Working while you collect

If you claim before full retirement age and keep working, Social Security holds back $1 for every $2 you earn over $24,480 (the 2026 limit).

That money isn't gone for good. Once you reach full retirement age, your monthly check goes up to make up for it. After full retirement age, there's no limit on what you can earn.

4The 3.6% raise for 2027

Social Security announced a 3.6% cost-of-living adjustment (COLA) for 2027. It applies to every claiming age, so it doesn't change which age is best.

The raise starts with January 2027 payments. Medicare premiums often go up in January too, so your deposit may rise a little less.

5Common questions

Can I change my mind after I claim?

Within 12 months, you can withdraw your claim once, but you must pay back what you got.

Is Social Security taxed?

Part of it can be, depending on your other income. Oregon doesn't tax Social Security, and Washington has no income tax.

Does claiming age affect SSI or SNAP?

Yes. Social Security counts as income for both, so a bigger check can lower them.
