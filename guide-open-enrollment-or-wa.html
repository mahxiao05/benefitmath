Open Enrollment 2027 in Oregon and Washington

Marketplace plans have a sign-up season. OHP and Apple Health don't. Here's what to do and when.

At a glance

Nov 1Open enrollment starts
Dec 15Sign up by this date for coverage on Jan 1
Any timeApply for OHP or Apple Health
  • Oregon has a new marketplace: Explore Health replaces HealthCare.gov on November 1, 2026.
  • Washington stays on Washington Healthplanfinder.
  • Prices changed. Compare plans instead of letting yours renew automatically.
DatesOregonWashingtonCost

1Key dates

DateWhat happens
Nov 1, 2026Open enrollment starts for 2027 plans
Dec 15, 2026Last day to sign up or switch for coverage starting January 1
Jan 15, 2027Open enrollment ends. Plans picked after Dec 15 start February 1

These are the federal dates. Confirm on your state's marketplace, which can set its own.

2Oregon: Explore Health

  • Shop at Explore Health starting November 1. It replaces HealthCare.gov for Oregon.
  • Have a 2026 HealthCare.gov plan? You'll get renewal information first. Then sign in to Explore Health to update your application and choose a plan.
  • Help: 855-268-3767 or info.marketplace@odhsoha.oregon.gov. Free local helpers are listed on the state's find-help page.
  • OHP: apply any time at benefits.oregon.gov or 800-699-9075. OHP Bridge covers some adults earning a bit more.
Advertisement

3Washington: Healthplanfinder

  • Shop at Washington Healthplanfinder. Help: 1-855-923-4633.
  • Cascade Care Savings is Washington's own help with premiums, on top of the federal tax credit, for people who qualify.
  • Apple Health: apply any time on Healthplanfinder. There's no enrollment season.

4What changed on cost

  • The extra federal help from 2021 to 2025 has expired. Tax credits again stop above 400% of the poverty level, and many people pay more than before.
  • Update your income when you renew. A lower estimate can mean more help now, but you'll settle up at tax time.
  • Check that your doctors and prescriptions are covered before you switch plans.
