Common questions

How do I qualify?
  1. 10 years: pay premiums for a total of 10 years, each with at least 500 hours of work. That earns the benefit for good.
  2. 3 of the last 6 years: pay premiums in at least 3 of the 6 years before you apply. This is the route most people can use now.
  3. Born before 1968: if neither route above applies, you earn one-tenth of the benefit for each year you paid.

You also need help with at least 3 of 7 daily activities (bathing, bed mobility, eating, managing medications, moving around, transferring, toileting) for 90 days or more. Needing supervision counts.

What does it pay for?

Services at home (personal care, home-delivered meals, housework and errands, home modifications, adaptive equipment, emergency response systems), adult day services and transportation, professional nursing, respite and dementia supports for family caregivers, and care in assisted living, adult family homes, memory care or nursing homes. Once you're approved, WA Cares pays the provider directly. WA Cares benefits

What if I move out of state?

WA Cares says you can move out of state and keep participating so you can use benefits when you need care.

How do I apply?

Online through SecureAccess Washington, by phone at 844-CARE4WA (844-227-3492), or in person through a local Area Agency on Aging.
