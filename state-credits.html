Common questions

New to this? Read The EITC in Oregon and Washington.

How Washington's credit is figured
  1. Start with the maximum for your number of children and tax year (for 2025: $335, $660, $995 or $1,330).
  2. If your income is within $2,500 (no children) or $5,000 (with children) of the federal EITC income limit, the credit shrinks by a set percentage of each dollar above that point.
  3. If you still qualify, you get at least $50.
Can I still claim past years?

Yes. Washington accepts claims for each tax year for about four years: 2022 through December 31, 2026, 2023 through 2027, 2024 through 2028 and 2025 through 2029. Each year needs its own application and that year's federal return.

Can I apply with an ITIN?

Yes, in both states. Washington accepts ITIN filers who meet the EITC rules other than the Social Security number requirement, and says it doesn't share citizenship or immigration status with federal authorities. Oregon lets ITIN filers claim its earned income credit on Schedule OR-EIC-ITIN.

Where do I apply?

Washington: at WorkingFamiliesCredit.wa.gov, on paper, or through participating tax software. It's a separate application, not part of a state tax return (Washington has no income tax). Oregon: claim both credits on your Oregon income tax return. You can file it free with Direct File Oregon.
