When the increase starts

What is the 2027 COLA?

3.6%. SSA announced it on October 14, 2026, based on inflation from July through September. It starts with January 2027 payments.

How is the COLA figured?

By law, it equals the increase in the Consumer Price Index for Urban Wage Earners and Clerical Workers (CPI-W) from the third quarter of last year to the third quarter of this year.

Does the COLA apply to SSI?

Yes. The SSI maximum goes up by the same percentage, rounded by SSA. Your SSI check also depends on your other income.

Will the COLA affect my SNAP or Medicaid?

It can. A bigger check counts as more income, which can lower SNAP slightly. See your new SNAP amount with the SNAP calculator.

What was the 2026 COLA?

2.8%, announced in October 2025. It raised the SSI maximum to $994 a month for one person.
