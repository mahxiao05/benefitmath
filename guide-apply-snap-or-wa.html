How to Apply for SNAP in Oregon and Washington

Five steps from application to your first deposit. You can start today with just your name and address.

At a glance

30 daysMost the state can take to decide
7 daysEmergency SNAP, if you qualify
1Short interview, usually by phone
  • You can file with only your name, address and signature. Finish the rest later.
  • Very low income or savings this month? Ask for expedited (emergency) SNAP.
  • Benefits come on an EBT card: the Oregon Trail Card or Washington Quest card.
StepsWhereWhat to haveEmergency SNAPAfter

1The five steps

  1. Check if you might qualify. Our calculator takes two minutes and nothing you type leaves your device.
  2. Apply. Online, by phone or in person (see below). Your filing date starts the clock, so send it even if it's not complete.
  3. Do your interview. The state will call or send you a time. Phone is the usual way. Can't make it? Call right away to reschedule.
  4. Send any proof they ask for. You'll get a list. Send it by the due date on the notice.
  5. Get your card. If you're approved, your EBT card comes by mail and benefits are loaded each month.

3What to have ready

You don't need these to apply, but having them speeds things up:

  • ID for the person applying
  • Pay stubs or other income from the last 30 days, for everyone in your household
  • Your rent or mortgage amount, and what you pay for utilities
  • Child care or child support you pay
  • If someone is 60+ or has a disability: medical bills you pay out of pocket

Rent, utilities and medical costs can raise your benefit, so list them even if you think they won't matter.

4Getting SNAP within 7 days

Federal rules require the state to get benefits to you within 7 days if any of these is true:

  • Your household's income this month is under $150, and you have $100 or less in cash and bank accounts
  • Your income plus cash on hand is less than your rent or mortgage plus utilities
  • You're a migrant or seasonal farmworker household with $100 or less

Say you need help right away when you apply. Washington says eligible households can sometimes get benefits the same day. Everyone else gets a decision within 30 days.

5After you're approved

  • Your approval letter shows your monthly amount and your deposit date.
  • Report changes the state asks you to report, such as a big income change.
  • Renew when you get a notice. If you're 18 to 64 without kids under 14, work rules may apply.
  • Stretch your benefits with what SNAP can buy, including farmers market matches.
