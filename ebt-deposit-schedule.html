If your benefits are late or missing

OregonCheck your balance at ebtedge.us, in the ebtEDGE app, or call 888-997-4447 (24 hours). If your card was turned off or you think it's fraud, call 800-699-9075.
WashingtonBenefits are on your card by 9 a.m. Pacific on your day, even on weekends and holidays. Check at ebtedge.com, in the ebtEDGE app, or call 888-328-9271.

Your first month, or the month after a renewal, can come on a different day. If you just applied, your first benefits may arrive separately.

Sources: ODHS EBT · DSHS 22-310

Keep your benefits safe

Lost or stolen cardOregon: 888-997-4447 any time, and 855-328-6715 for a replacement on weekdays. Washington: 888-328-9271.
Card skimmingIf benefits disappear, cancel your card right away. In Oregon, stolen cash (TANF) can be replaced if you report it within 30 days.
Use them or lose themOregon removes benefits you haven't used within a year. Washington cancels benefits after 274 days without use.
Change your PIN oftenYou can change it at ebtedge.us or in the ebtEDGE app. Never share it.

Common questions

When do Oregon food stamps come?

Between the 1st and the 9th of every month. The day matches the last digit of the head of household's Social Security number: 0 or 1 on the 1st, 2 on the 2nd, and so on up to 9 on the 9th.

When does SNAP come in Washington?

On the same day every month, by the 20th. Washington assigns your day when you're approved and lists it on your approval letter. Benefits are available by 9 a.m. Pacific, even on weekends and holidays.

Do benefits come early on weekends or holidays?

No. They load on your usual day either way. In Washington, DSHS says benefits are deposited on the same day even on weekends and holidays.

When does Oregon cash assistance (TANF) come?

On the 1st of each month.

Why did my first payment come on a different day?

The first month of a new or reopened case is handled separately. After that, benefits follow the regular schedule.

Do unused benefits roll over?

Yes. In Oregon they stay on your card for up to a year. In Washington, benefits not used for 274 days are canceled.
