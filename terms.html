Terms of Use and Disclaimer

The plain-language rules for using this site.

Last updated October 12, 2026

Estimates, not decisions

Every result on BenefitMath is an estimate based on published rules and the numbers you enter. Only the agency that runs a program can decide whether you qualify and how much you receive. Agencies consider details a calculator can't, such as verified documents and exactly when income arrives. If an estimate says you don't qualify, you can still apply.

Not legal, tax or financial advice

The site explains how rules work. It is not legal advice, tax advice or financial advice, and it doesn't create any professional relationship. For decisions with real consequences, like when to claim Social Security or how to file a tax return, check with the agency or a qualified professional. Free tax help is available through the IRS VITA program.
