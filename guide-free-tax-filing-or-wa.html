Free Ways to File Your Taxes in Oregon and Washington

IRS Direct File is gone, but you can still file both federal and Oregon returns for free.

At a glance

$89,000Income limit for IRS Free File software
~$70,244Income most VITA volunteers serve
$0Direct File Oregon, for most full-year residents
  • Do it yourself: IRS Free File for your federal return, Direct File Oregon for your state return.
  • Want help? Free volunteer programs prepare returns for people with moderate income.
  • Washington has no income tax, but its Working Families credit has its own free application.

3What happened to IRS Direct File?

The IRS's own free filing program isn't available anymore and isn't coming back for the 2026 filing season. Direct File Oregon still works, but you now enter your federal return information by hand.

4Washington: don't skip the credit

Washington has no income tax return, but its Working Families Tax Credit is a separate, free application at workingfamiliescredit.wa.gov, on paper, or through participating tax software. You'll need your federal return filed first.

5What to bring

  • Photo ID and Social Security cards or ITIN letters for everyone on the return
  • W-2s, 1099s and any other income forms
  • Last year's return, if you have it
  • Bank account and routing numbers for direct deposit
