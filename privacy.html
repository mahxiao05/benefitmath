Privacy Policy

Short version: what you type stays on your device. Ads may use cookies, and you can opt out.

Last updated October 14, 2026

What you type stays on your device

All calculations happen inside your web browser. The numbers you enter (income, age, household size, ratings and so on) are never sent to BenefitMath or stored on any server. There are no accounts, and the calculators never ask for your name, email, or contact details. The only place you can give an email address is the optional email updates signup described below.

To save you from retyping, each calculator remembers your last entries using your browser's local storage. That information exists only on your device. You can erase it anytime by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Advertising and cookies

BenefitMath is free because it shows ads. Some tax pages may also list paid filing software through labeled partner links; if you use one, the company may pay BenefitMath a commission and may set its own cookies under its own privacy policy. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.
  • Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, through a consent message provided by Google. You can change your choice at any time from the privacy link that message adds to the page.

Ad partners may collect information such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited, under their own privacy policies. BenefitMath doesn't receive or see the numbers you enter into calculators, and never shares them with advertisers.

Hosting and outside links

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (like IP addresses) for security. Fonts load from Google Fonts. Links to government and other sites take you off BenefitMath, and their own privacy policies apply there.

Email updates

If you sign up for email alerts, the only thing we collect is your email address. It is stored by our email service provider and used only to send BenefitMath updates, like changes to benefit or tax rules. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and we never sell or share your address. Nothing you type into a calculator is attached to it.

Children

BenefitMath is meant for adults and is not directed to children under 13. It doesn't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated.

Questions about this policy? See the contact page or email info@benefitmath.com. If you email us, we use your message only to reply.
